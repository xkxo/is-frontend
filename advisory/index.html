Who We Are

An advisory practice built for growing businesses

InfoSurge is an integration and CRM advisory practice built for growing businesses — the ones that have outgrown spreadsheets and manual workarounds, but aren't ready for (or don't need) an enterprise-scale rebuild.

We work directly with your team to clean up your CRM, connect the systems around it, and build the integration layer — CRM, ERP, e-commerce, EDI, and everything in between — that lets information move on its own. Every engagement is hands-on, senior-led, and scoped to the size of the business we're working with.

Built around one idea

"Systems should work together quietly, in the background — not become a full-time job for your team."

Our Core Belief

Data comes first. Before everything else.

Data is what gets your business paid.

An invoice can't go out on bad data. An order can't ship on bad data. A partner won't reconcile against bad data. Every integration, every automation, every dashboard we build sits on top of the data underneath it — so if the data is wrong, everything built on it is wrong too. That's why we fix data quality first, always, before we automate or connect anything else. It's not a step we get to eventually. It's where we start.

The Problem We Solve

When systems don't talk to each other

The cost shows up in cash flow — not just convenience.

Disconnected Systems

CRM, accounting, e-commerce, and ERP each hold a different version of the truth.

Manual Re-Entry

Staff re-key the same order or invoice into three systems, and errors creep in every time.

Delayed Invoicing

Bad or late data means invoices go out late — and payment comes in later still.

Reporting Blind Spots

Leadership makes decisions on numbers nobody fully trusts.
