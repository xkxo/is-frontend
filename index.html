About us

Integration specialists, not generalists.

Most businesses don't have a software problem. They have a connection problem: orders re-keyed from emails, spreadsheets passed between teams, and customers asking for EDI you can't yet send. Infosurge is a Birmingham-based integration and EDI consultancy. We connect your systems to each other and to your trading partners, check every document before it leaves, and stay on hand once you're live.

What we do

Built to connect, built to last.

From your first trading partner to your fiftieth, one team handles the mapping, the testing and the support.

EDI & partner onboarding

Get trading electronically with retailers, carmakers and NHS trusts. We work from your partner's specification, build and test the messages, and see you through partner testing to go-live.

System integration

Connect your ERP, accounts, warehouse, e-commerce and carrier systems. Data is entered once, flows everywhere it's needed, and is checked on the way.

Custom solutions

Where off-the-shelf tools fall short, we build what's missing as part of your integration project: CRM set-ups, dashboards, portals and automations, designed around your data.
